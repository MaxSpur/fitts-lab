Methods & data

Plots show first attempts, including misses. Means and linear fits use successful first attempts. Fits are pooled across students, with separate lines for each stage, recorded device category, cursor mode, gain, jitter setting, and protocol version. Students with more observations or repeat runs contribute more to the fit. R² describes the displayed fit; it is not a confidence interval.

Boundary comparisons match free/edge sets within the same student run. Free comes first, so practice may affect the difference. Edge targets are excluded from Fitts regressions. Very large classes display a sample of dots; calculations and exports retain all observations.

Freeze pauses the display while collection continues. Local rehearsal works between tabs in this browser; a shared classroom needs Supabase. Simulated data stays separate from real sessions.
